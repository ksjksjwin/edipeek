Contact

EdiPeek is an independent project, and feedback is always welcome. Whether you have found a bug, want a new EDI transaction type supported, or have a general question, you can reach out by email.

Email

The best way to reach us:

📧 hello@edipeek.com

We read every message and try to respond within a few business days.

Good things to include

  • For a bug: what you pasted (with any sensitive data removed), and what went wrong.
  • For a feature request: the EDI standard (X12 or EDIFACT) and the transaction/message type you would like supported.
  • For general feedback: anything you found confusing or anything you would like to see.

A note on privacy

Please do not email us real, unredacted EDI files containing confidential or personal data. Remember that our tool itself never uploads your files — they are parsed entirely in your browser. See our privacy policy for details.
