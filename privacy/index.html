Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

EdiPeek ("we", "us", "the site") operates the free EDI viewing tools available at edipeek.com. This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use the site.

The short version

The EDI files you paste into our tools are never uploaded to any server. All parsing happens locally inside your own web browser using JavaScript. We never see, store, or transmit the contents of your files.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit the EDI data you paste into the viewers.
  • We do not require accounts, logins, or any sign-up.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal details to use the tools.

Information that may be collected automatically

Like most websites, basic technical information may be logged by our hosting provider and analytics, such as your browser type, device type, approximate region, and the pages you visit. This is used only to understand general usage and keep the site running. It is not linked to your identity.

Cookies and advertising

We use third-party advertising, including Google AdSense, to support the free operation of this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of third-party services may not work as a result.

Third-party links

Our site may link to external resources (for example, standards documentation). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

Children's privacy

This site is a technical tool intended for business and professional use. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
